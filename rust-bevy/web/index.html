WHAT IS ACTUALLY RENDERED

Built to be measured.

Real Bevy 0.16.1 compiled to WebAssembly, with wgpu targeting browser WebGPU. No WebGL fallback is included; unsupported browsers show an explicit message.

  • Faceted procedural crystal meshes with a custom WGSL PBR extension: animated emissive interference bands and view-dependent spectral color.
  • One shadow-casting directional light, two colored point lights, metallic materials, distance fog, HDR bloom and Tony McMapface tone mapping.
  • CPU-driven crystal and particle animation. Shared meshes and materials permit Bevy batching; no custom compute simulation or ray tracing is claimed.
  • Frame rate is browser requestAnimationFrame cadence. GPU timestamps, draw-call counts and VRAM are not measured. Triangle count estimates active scene geometry before culling, excluding shadow passes.
  • Spawning is limited to 96 crystals per frame. Allocated crystals are retained and hidden when load is lowered. Adaptive resolution targets 30 FPS with a 50% floor; it cannot guarantee performance.
